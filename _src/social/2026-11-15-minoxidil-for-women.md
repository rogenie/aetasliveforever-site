Is minoxidil worth it for thinning hair in women, and is the pill better than the foam?

1. Topical minoxidil is the only FDA-approved medicine for female pattern hair loss. In a 48-week trial of 381 women, both 5% and 2% beat placebo. (Evidence: human trial)
2. Extra shedding in the first couple of weeks is expected, results take months, and regrown hair is usually lost within months of stopping. (Evidence: human trial)
3. Low-dose oral minoxidil is off-label for hair loss. Small trials in women look promising, but none used a placebo, and unwanted facial and body hair is its most common side effect. (Evidence: review)
