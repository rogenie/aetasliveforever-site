Your ferritin came back "normal," so why is your hair still shedding?

1. A 2022 analysis of 36 studies found women with non-scarring hair loss had lower ferritin (stored iron) on average. That's a link, not proof iron fixes hair. (Evidence: human observational)
2. Cutoffs differ: one common lab range starts at 13 ng/mL, a clinical review uses below 30, and a gastroenterology guideline uses below 45 for people with anemia. (Evidence: review)
3. Inflammation can push ferritin up and hide low iron, and taking iron without testing carries real risks, including iron overload and medicine interactions. (Evidence: review)
