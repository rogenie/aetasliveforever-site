Hair coming out in handfuls a few months after a fever, surgery or a new baby? It may be telogen effluvium.

1. Shedding usually starts about 3 months after the trigger (range 1-6 months), so the link is easy to miss. (Evidence: review)
2. The acute form usually settles once the trigger is gone. Regrowth can take up to 6 months to restart and full density several months to a year. (Evidence: review)
3. Shedding past 6 months is called chronic. A 1996 biopsy study of 355 people (346 women) found it mostly in women 30-60, distinct from pattern hair loss. (Evidence: human observational)
