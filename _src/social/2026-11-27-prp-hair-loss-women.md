Does PRP actually help thinning hair in women?

1. In a randomized trial of 30 women with pattern thinning, three monthly PRP injections improved hair density and thickness more than saltwater injections at 24 weeks. The trial was funded by the maker of the PRP system it tested. (Evidence: human trial)
2. A 2024 review of 21 studies in 628 women also favored PRP, but results varied widely between studies and almost none followed women past six months. (Evidence: review)
3. There's no standard PRP recipe, and PRP is not FDA-approved for hair loss; using it for hair is off-label. (Evidence: review)
