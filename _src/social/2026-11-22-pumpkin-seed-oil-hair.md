Does pumpkin seed oil actually help thinning hair?

1. The key trial gave 76 men with pattern hair loss a pumpkin seed oil capsule or a placebo for 24 weeks. Hair counts rose 40% vs. 10% on placebo, but hair didn't get thicker. (Evidence: human trial)
2. No women were in that trial, it was funded by the capsule's maker, and it hasn't been repeated. (Evidence: human trial)
3. The one trial in women compared scalp-applied pumpkin seed oil with minoxidil foam, with no placebo group, so it can't show what the oil itself did. (Evidence: human trial)
