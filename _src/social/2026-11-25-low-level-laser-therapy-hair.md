Do laser caps and combs actually help thinning hair?

1. In two sham-controlled trials in women with pattern thinning, a red-light laser comb used three times a week for 26 weeks added about 20 hairs per square centimeter, versus about 3 with a look-alike sham. (Evidence: human trial)
2. A 2021 review of 7 randomized trials also favored the devices in men and women, but the trials were short and mostly tied to device makers. (Evidence: review)
3. "FDA-cleared" means a device resembles one already sold. Many cleared devices were never tested in their own trial. (Evidence: official FDA records)
