Heard spironolactone can help thinning hair? Here's what the studies show.

1. It's a 1960s heart and blood pressure drug used off-label for female pattern hair loss. It isn't FDA-approved for hair. (Evidence: official FDA label)
2. A 2023 meta-analysis of 5 studies found 56.6% of patients improved, more when combined with minoxidil. Only one study was a randomized trial. (Evidence: review)
3. It needs potassium and kidney checks, and the FDA label says to avoid it in pregnancy because of possible effects on a male fetus. (Evidence: official FDA label)
