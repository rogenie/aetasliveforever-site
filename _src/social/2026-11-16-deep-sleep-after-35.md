Waking at 3 a.m. in your 40s? Here's what's actually changing with your deep sleep.

1. Deep sleep slowly shrinks across adulthood. A 2004 analysis of 65 sleep-lab studies found it's a normal part of aging. (Evidence: human observational)
2. Two sleep-lab studies that followed women through menopause didn't find it wiped out deep sleep. The bigger change is waking up more, often tied to hot flashes. (Evidence: human observational)
3. In a 2016 trial of 106 women aged 40-65 with hot flashes, 8 weeks of phone-based CBT-I left 70% in the no-insomnia range, vs 24% with menopause education. (Evidence: human trial)
