Taking biotin for thinning hair? Here's what the research shows.

1. A 2024 review found no studies showing biotin improves hair growth in healthy people. The only placebo-controlled trial (46 women, 1966) found no difference. (Evidence: review)
2. Biotin deficiency is rare in people eating a normal diet, per the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements. Where biotin helped hair, there was an underlying condition. (Evidence: review)
3. FDA warns high-dose biotin can cause falsely high or low results on some lab tests, including troponin (a heart attack test) and thyroid tests. Tell your clinician and lab if you take it. (Evidence: official FDA guidance)
