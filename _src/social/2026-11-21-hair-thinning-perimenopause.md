Wider part, thinner ponytail in your 40s? Here's what's behind hair thinning in perimenopause.

1. Female pattern hair loss commonly shows up around menopause, and falling estrogen is thought to play a part, per a 2022 dermatology review. Most women have normal androgen levels. (Evidence: review)
2. Thyroid disease and iron deficiency are worth ruling out before buying products. (Evidence: official guidance)
3. Topical minoxidil is the only FDA-approved medicine for female pattern hair loss. Results can take 3 to 6 months. The review found insufficient evidence for HRT as a hair treatment. (Evidence: review)
