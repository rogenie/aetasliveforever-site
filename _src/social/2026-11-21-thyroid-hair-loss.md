Thinning hair and tired all the time? Your thyroid is worth asking about.

1. NIH lists dry, thinning hair among the symptoms of an underactive thyroid, and one in eight women will develop a thyroid problem in her lifetime, per the US Office on Women's Health. (Evidence: official guidance)
2. In a 2024 study of 500 women with sudden shedding, 252 had an underactive or overactive thyroid. It was one clinic looking back at records, so it can't predict your odds. (Evidence: human observational)
3. Symptoms can't diagnose it. The usual first test is TSH, often followed by T4. High-dose biotin can skew thyroid results, so mention it. (Evidence: official guidance)
