What is zone 2 cardio, and how much do you actually need?

1. Zone 2 is steady cardio where you can talk but not sing. There's no single agreed definition, and fixed heart-rate percentages may not match your own threshold. (Evidence: human study)
2. US guidelines call for 150 to 300 minutes a week of moderate activity, or 75 to 150 of vigorous, plus strength work 2 or more days. (Evidence: official guideline)
3. Steady cardio does grow mitochondria, but in a review of hundreds of studies, interval training did it at least as well in less time. Zone 2 isn't magic. (Evidence: review)
